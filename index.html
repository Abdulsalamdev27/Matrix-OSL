Who We Are

Local knowledge. Certified people. Dependable delivery.

Matrix Oilfield Services Limited is an independently owned oilfield services company headquartered in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. We support operators and EPC contractors across the Niger Delta with well services, pipeline integrity and HSE advisory support. Our field teams combine international certification standards with knowledge of local operating conditions.

Health, Safety & Environment

Zero Compromise. Every Operation.

Our goal-zero approach is supported by daily toolbox talks, documented permit-to-work systems and stop-work authority for every person on site, regardless of role or seniority.

Our HSE function reports directly to executive management, independently of operations, so safety decisions are not subordinated to schedule or cost pressures.
